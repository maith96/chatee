## Ready-made application answers

**Company:** Sasamigo, by Leinalok Labs (Nairobi, Kenya)
**Website:** sasamigo.com · **Email:** hello@sasamigo.com
**Stage:** Product built and live; preparing a closed beta in Nairobi.

**One-liner (≤20 words):**
Sasamigo is AI friends who remember your life, text you first and know what's happening in Kenya, always
honestly AI.

**Problem (≤60 words):**
Young Kenyans live on their phones, but AI companion apps weren't built for them. They forget you between
chats, they're built in the US and priced in dollars, and the biggest ones face lawsuits and privacy fines for
hooking users. Nobody offers AI friendship that knows East African life and is honest and safe by design.

**Solution (≤80 words):**
Sasamigo is a texting app with AI friends who feel like real friends. They remember what you tell them (and you
can see and delete those memories), check in after a quiet spell, bring up today's news including Kenya, and
text in short, natural bubbles with light Sheng. AI "strangers" can say hi, at most once a day and never at
night. Every character is labelled AI, it's adults only, and crisis messages always show Kenyan helplines.

**What's built (≤80 words):**
A live web app and public REST API (Next.js, Supabase, NVIDIA NIM with Google Gemini as automatic backup).
Five characters plus a pool of AI strangers with matching; long-term memory with a grounding check; check-ins;
a daily news digest; per-user style learning; 18+ age check; crisis detection; account deletion; privacy policy
and terms written for Kenya's Data Protection Act; automated conversation evals with a judge model.

**Business model (≤50 words):**
Freemium. Free daily messages; then small daily, weekly or monthly bundles paid with M-Pesa (no card needed).
Safety features are never paywalled.
